Dedication

A quiet offering of gratitude, surrender, and honor to the One who remained faithful through every season.

To Jesus, the name above every name, the One who remained faithful through every hidden season, every wilderness road, every unanswered prayer, and every moment where strength felt small.

Thank You for speaking within quiet places when life became loud. Thank You for staying near during seasons where understanding felt distant. Thank You for teaching lessons through waiting, surrender, tears, and grace.

This book was never written for a large name, recognition, or applause. These pages belong to You.

Keep hearts teachable. Keep spirits willing. Keep ears attentive. Keep lives prepared in season and out. Keep every reader small enough to hear You.

No big name. Just a willing vessel.
