Preface

A quiet beginning written through surrender, hidden seasons, prayer, and learning to remain close to Jesus.

Books often begin with a reason, though some begin with a prayer. This one began quietly.

No dream ever lived within my heart to build a large name, create a platform, or become known. Recognition was never the pursuit. Attention never felt like the destination.

A different desire continued growing through seasons of prayer, waiting, learning, and walking with Jesus. Deep within my heart, a simple prayer remained: Lord, keep me willing.

Life has a way of teaching lessons through unexpected places. Certain truths are learned in hidden seasons where applause never reaches. Some lessons are discovered during quiet nights filled with prayer. Others grow through wilderness roads, unanswered questions, and moments where heaven feels silent.

A simple image continued returning to my heart through those seasons: an ant. Small, often unnoticed, yet faithful and prepared. That image slowly became a prayer: Jesus, keep me small enough to hear You.

These pages were never written from a place of having every answer. The journey ahead was written through surrender, healing, waiting, and discovering that Jesus still speaks in quiet places.
