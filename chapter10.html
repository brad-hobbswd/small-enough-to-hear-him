Chapter Ten

There Is a Name I Know

Holding tightly to the name of Jesus through every storm, battle, and uncertain season.

Life has a way of revealing what hearts truly trust.

Certain seasons feel stable and peaceful. Other seasons shake everything familiar. Fear can suddenly appear through unexpected loss, uncertainty, sickness, disappointment, or battles that seem larger than personal strength.

Storms often expose where security has been placed.

“There is a name stronger than every fear.”

Many people spend years searching for peace through temporary things. Some trust money. Others trust control, approval, relationships, success, or personal ability. Difficult seasons eventually reveal that human strength alone cannot carry every burden.

One name remains unshaken through every season. Jesus.

The name of Jesus carries power far beyond human understanding. Scripture repeatedly reveals lives transformed through His name. Blind eyes opened. Chains broke. Fear lost authority. Hope returned. Storms calmed.

The power never rested in human ability. The power rested in Jesus.

Acts 4:12 says:

“Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.”

That name still carries authority today.

Many people know what it feels like to reach moments where strength feels small. Nights can become heavy. Thoughts can become overwhelming. Certain battles leave hearts exhausted and unsure how to continue forward.

Even there, the name of Jesus remains near.

A willing vessel learns something powerful over time. Peace does not always come from understanding every situation. Peace often comes from remembering who Jesus is within every situation.

Jesus remains faithful during uncertainty. Jesus remains present during grief. Jesus remains strong when hearts feel weak.

Many prayers become beautifully simple during difficult seasons:

Jesus, help me. Jesus, keep me steady. Jesus, stay near.

He hears every sincere cry.

The enemy often attempts to magnify fear until problems appear larger than God’s promises. Fear grows loud. Anxiety grows heavy. Doubt attempts to cloud vision.

The name of Jesus still speaks peace.

No darkness outruns His presence. No burden overwhelms His strength. No storm removes His authority.

Many people search for complicated answers while heaven repeatedly points toward one simple truth:

Jesus is enough.

A willing vessel does not place ultimate trust in personal strength, human systems, or temporary stability. A willing vessel learns to anchor deeply within the name above every name.

Small enough to hear Him means remembering that through every valley, every storm, and every uncertain season, there is still a name that saves, restores, strengthens, and remains faithful.

Jesus.
