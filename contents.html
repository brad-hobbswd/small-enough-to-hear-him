No big name. Just a willing vessel. A quiet journey through surrender, hidden faithfulness, listening, and learning to stay close enough to hear the voice of Jesus.

Book Contents

Each chapter walks through hidden seasons, quiet surrender, wilderness roads, and the beauty of remaining willing in the hands of God. Written with a reflective and immersive tone, this journey invites hearts back toward stillness, humility, and the voice of Jesus.
