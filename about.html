About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

A quiet journey of surrender, faithfulness, and learning to remain close enough to hear the voice of Jesus.

Bradley Hobbs writes from a place of faith, surrender, and a desire to remain a willing vessel in the hands of Jesus. Rather than pursuing recognition or building a large platform, his heart has continually been drawn toward a quieter prayer: Lord, keep me willing. Keep me listening. Keep me small enough to hear You.

Much of his writing grows from hidden places where lessons are learned through prayer, waiting seasons, healing, and the everyday journey of walking with God. Through deeply personal reflections and spiritually grounded messages, Bradley shares themes of humility, surrender, faithfulness, and the life changing power found in the name of Jesus.

His passion is not centered around becoming known. A greater desire lives within these pages: helping others draw closer to Jesus and reminding hearts that God still speaks within quiet places. Hidden seasons are never wasted, and willing vessels remain precious within the hands of God.

When titles fade and recognition pass away, one prayer continues guiding the journey: No big name. Just a willing vessel. Still small enough to hear Him.
