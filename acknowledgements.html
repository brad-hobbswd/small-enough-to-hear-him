Acknowledgements

Gratitude for every prayer, every quiet encouragement, and every moment of grace that carried this journey forward.

First and above everything else, thank You Jesus.

Thank You for remaining faithful through every valley, every wilderness season, every hidden tear, and every quiet moment where Your presence carried strength into weary places. None of these pages would exist without Your mercy, patience, and endless grace.

“Jesus remained faithful through every chapter.”

Thank you to every person who continued praying even during unseen seasons. Quiet prayers carry eternal weight. Encouragement spoken during difficult moments often reaches farther than people realize.

Thank you to family who continued walking beside the journey. Thank you for patience, kindness, and support during long writing days, reflective nights, and moments where words slowly formed chapter by chapter.

Thank you to every believer who continues choosing humility, surrender, and closeness with Jesus within a world constantly chasing visibility and noise. Quiet faithfulness still matters deeply.

Many people may never fully understand hidden obedience, yet heaven sees every surrendered moment offered from love.

Thank you to every reader opening these pages with a sincere heart. Prayer remains that every chapter gently points hearts toward the peace, nearness, and voice of Jesus.

No large name. No performance. No striving for recognition.

Just a willing vessel continuing to learn how to remain small enough to hear Him.

Jesus deserves every ounce of glory.
